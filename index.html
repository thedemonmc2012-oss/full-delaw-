Quick Study Tips

Break big topics into small parts. Study 25 minutes, then rest 5 minutes.
Teach the topic to someone else (or to Delaw). Teaching helps you remember.
Write short notes in your own words instead of copying everything.
Sleep well. Your brain organizes what you learned while you sleep.
Review your notes the next day. Spaced repetition is powerful.
